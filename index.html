The kirana

Local stores deserve digital reach.

  • Limited online visibility.
  • Difficulty receiving digital orders at scale.
  • Need for clearer inventory and fulfilment workflows.

Local at heart. Smart by design.

1. Discover

Customers browse groceries and products available through participating local stores.

2. Match

KiranaBolt identifies a suitable store based on availability, distance, service capability, and order economics.

3. Prepare

The selected store receives the order and prepares the items.

4. Deliver

A delivery partner transports the order to the customer.

CUSTOMER → SMART MATCHING → LOCAL STORE → DELIVERY

The smart matching engine

Every order finds its local match.

Our proposed matching system is designed to consider availability, distance, service reliability, and fulfilment economics when selecting a suitable store.
